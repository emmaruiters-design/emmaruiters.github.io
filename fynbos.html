Fynbos needs fire to grow

Fynbos is the smallest and most diverse floral kingdom on earth, and it grows nowhere else than the west coast of South Africa.

Many fynbos species will not release their seed until a fire has passed through - literally born again in fire.

In the same way, African businesses thrive in challenging environments, are unique to African economies, and when they prosper a thousand flowers bloom.
