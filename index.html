Perspective

I believe Africa’s next phase of growth will be shaped by its ability to build technological capability, mobilise domestic capital and forge global partnerships that create shared value.

  1. Sovereign Technology & Innovation Capability

    The challenge facing African countries is not a lack of technological talent or entrepreneurial capability, but the structural constraints that prevent these ecosystems from translating that capability into sovereign technology at scale.

  2. Building Domestic Capital and Systems

    Africa is often viewed as dependent on external capital, but this overlooks the growing depth of its domestic funding ecosystems. While these markets remain relatively nascent, domestic pools of capital are expanding and becoming more sophisticated, creating an increasingly important foundation for African-led growth alongside international investment.

  3. Global Economic Partnership and Diplomacy: Africa, the UK and Japan

    The next phase of global prosperity should be built on demographic complementarity: capital-rich economies facing ageing populations investing in the young, expanding economies of the Global South, and sharing in the growth they help unlock. The opportunity is not simply to transfer capital from North to South, but to create a mutually reinforcing cycle in which investment accelerates productivity and prosperity in emerging economies while generating new sources of growth and returns for global capital.
